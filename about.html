Mission, Vision & Goals
🎯
Our Mission

To provide the Cavite State University community with a reliable, centralized digital platform that simplifies the recovery of lost items. We aim to bridge the gap between finders and owners through technology, fostering a culture of honesty and proactive assistance within the campus.

🌿
Our Vision

To become the standard for campus integrity and digital community support at CvSU, where no lost item goes unreturned and every Kabsuhenyos feels empowered to contribute to a safer, more honest university environment.

⚡
Our Goals
Centralization

To eliminate the need for scattered social media posts by providing one dedicated hub for all CvSU campuses.

Efficiency

To reduce the time it takes to reunite lost items with their rightful owners through real-time updates.

Integrity

To promote the core CvSU value of "Character" by rewarding and highlighting acts of honesty across the community.

SDG
Sustainable Cities and Communities

Project Background
About PR🔍JECT #Ginhawa

PR🔍JECT #Ginhawa is a specialized web platform developed as a final project for the Computer Science department. Born from the need for a more organized way to handle lost property on campus, this system serves as a digital safety net for students, faculty, and staff. Unlike traditional bulletin boards, PR🔍JECT #Ginhawa provides real-time updates and direct contact lines to ensure that "Oops!" moments turn into "Found" success stories.
