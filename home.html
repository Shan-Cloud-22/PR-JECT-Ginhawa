About the Project

Ang Pambansang Lost and Found ng CvSU.

PR🔍JECT #Ginhawa is the official, centralized digital lost-and-found hub designed exclusively for the Cavite State University community. Whether you're a student who left a calculator in the Quadrangle, a teacher who found a flash drive in the faculty room, or an alumnus visiting the campus, this platform is your go-to safety net.

🛡️
Exclusivity

Only for Kabsuhenyos — Students, Teachers, Workers, Alumni. Outsiders are welcome 👋

⭐
Integrity

Built on the CvSU value of "Character" — encouraging honesty in returning found items.

⚡
Efficiency

No more scanning endless Facebook groups — everything is here, all in one place.

The Process

The 6-Step Kabsuhenyo Journey

1
Access the Site

Land on the PR🔍JECT #Ginhawa URL. The system welcomes you with that warm Cream background and Soft Tech layout — your entry point to the community safety net.

2
Explore the Hub

Scroll through the Home Page to get the full picture. View the Hero Section, learn What is PR🔍JECT #Ginhawa, and see the Community Impact. Trust before login.

3
Join the Community

Hit Sign Up — no @cvsu.edu.ph email required! Provide your Name, College, and basic details. Log In to unlock your private access and personal profile.

4
Manage Your Dashboard

Access your Personal Dashboard with a vertical sidebar. Create Reports for Lost or Found items. Check Updates to change item status — "Returned" or "Still Searching."

5
Browse the Centralized Feed

Head to All Items Page — every item card shows the Reporter's Name and Social Media links. Direct communication, no middleman. Kabsuhenyos helping Kabsuhenyos.

6
Meet the Vision

Visit the About Us page. Read the Mission, Vision, and Goals of PR🔍JECT #Ginhawa. See the Developer Team section and the faces behind the system.

Need urgent help? Lost valuables? Found something just now?

Browse all lost and found reports, filter by category, and connect directly with Kabsuhenyos who can help.
